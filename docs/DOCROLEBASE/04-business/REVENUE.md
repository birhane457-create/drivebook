# Business Revenue

**Route:** `/dashboard/revenue`  
**Auth required:** INSTRUCTOR role + BUSINESS tier  

---

## Purpose

Aggregated revenue reporting across all team instructors for the school owner.

---

## What It Shows

- Total revenue (all instructors combined)
- Revenue per instructor breakdown
- Commission paid to platform per instructor
- Net payout per instructor
- Date range filter (this week, this month, custom)

---

## Export

Revenue data can be exported as CSV for accounting purposes.
