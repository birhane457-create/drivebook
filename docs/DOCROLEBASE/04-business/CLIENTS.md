# Business — Client Management

**Route:** `/dashboard/clients` (BUSINESS tier view)  
**Auth required:** INSTRUCTOR role + BUSINESS tier  

---

## Purpose

BUSINESS tier accounts see clients across all team instructors, not just their own.

---

## What It Shows

- All clients linked to any instructor in the school
- Filter by instructor
- Client booking history across instructors
- Wallet balances

---

## Assigning Clients

School admins can reassign a client from one team instructor to another (e.g. if an instructor leaves).
