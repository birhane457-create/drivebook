# Quick Start Guide

## 30-Second Overview

**DriveBook connects driving students with instructors.**

**Students:**
1. Sign up → Find instructor → Book lesson → Pay → Take lesson → Review

**Instructors:**
1. Sign up → Get approved → Set availability → Accept bookings → Teach → Get paid

---

## I'm a Student — Get Started

### 1. Sign Up (2 min)
- Go to drivebook.com.au
- Click "Sign Up"
- Choose "Student"
- Enter email, password, name, phone
- Verify your email

### 2. Find an Instructor (5 min)
- Click "Browse Instructors"
- Filter by location, price, or reviews
- Click on instructor profile
- Read their bio and reviews
- Click "Book a Lesson"

### 3. Book Your First Lesson (3 min)
- Pick date and time (calendar shows available slots)
- Choose lesson type (Platform or Offline)
- Add any notes/special requests
- Review price
- Enter card details and confirm

### 4. Get Ready for Your Lesson
- Instructor will accept within 24 hours
- You'll get an email with location/details
- Show up on time!
- After lesson: leave a review

**Total time to first booking: ~15 minutes**

---

## I'm an Instructor — Get Started

### 1. Sign Up & Get Approved (5 min)
- Go to drivebook.com.au
- Click "Sign Up"
- Choose "Instructor"
- Enter email, password, name, phone
- Upload ID/documents
- ⏳ Wait 2-5 business days for approval

### 2. Set Up Your Profile (5 min)
- Add professional photo
- Write your bio
- Set your hourly rate
- Choose subscription tier (Basic/Pro/Business)

### 3. Set Your Availability (3 min)
- Go to "Availability"
- Click the times you can teach
- Save
- ✅ Now students can book you!

### 4. Set Up Payouts (2 min)
- Go to "Payout & Tax Settings"
- Click "Connect with Stripe →"
- Complete Stripe verification
- Your bank account is now connected

### 5. Accept Bookings & Teach
- When students book → You get an email
- Review the booking
- Click "Accept"
- Meet the student at scheduled time
- Get paid automatically every Tuesday

**Total time to first booking: ~15 minutes (plus 2-5 day approval)**

## Important Things to Know

### For Students

**Booking statuses:**
- 🟡 Pending = Instructor hasn't responded yet
- 🟢 Confirmed = Instructor accepted, lesson is scheduled
- ✅ Completed = Lesson finished
- ❌ Cancelled = Booking was cancelled

**Payment:**
- Card details are secure (through Stripe)
- You get a receipt via email
- Refund policy depends on cancellation timing

**Cancellation:**
- Cancel before confirmation = Full refund
- Cancel after confirmation = May lose payment
- Cancel within 24 hours = Penalty may apply

### For Instructors

**Approval:**
- Upload ALL documents immediately
- Don't wait — sooner you upload = sooner you're approved
- Admin reviews within 2-5 business days
- You can't accept bookings until approved

**Earnings:**
- You get paid every Tuesday automatically
- Earnings = Lesson price minus commission
- Commission varies by tier (10-15%)
- No minimum payout

**Communication:**
- Respond to bookings within 24 hours
- Respond to student messages quickly
- Professional communication = better ratings = more bookings

**Ratings matter:**
- 4.5+ stars = You rank higher in search
- Lower rating = Fewer booking requests
- Treat every student well!

## Payment & Pricing

### Student Payments
- **Accepted:** Credit card, Debit card
- **Charged:** When booking is confirmed
- **Safety:** Stripe handles all payments (secure)
- **Refund:** Via same card within 3-5 business days

### Instructor Payouts
- **Frequency:** Every Tuesday (automatic)
- **Method:** Direct transfer to your bank account
- **Timeframe:** 1-2 business days to appear
- **Setup:** Takes 2-3 minutes (Stripe Connect)
- **Minimum:** No minimum payout amount

### Pricing By Instructor
- Each instructor sets their own rate
- Range: $25-70/hour (depending on experience)
- You can see rates before booking
- Higher-rated instructors often charge more

## Problem? Here's What To Do

### Can't Log In?
1. Click "Forgot Password"
2. Enter your email
3. Check your email for reset link
4. Create new password
5. Log in with new password

### Booking Disappeared?
1. Go to "My Bookings"
2. Click "Show Cancelled"
3. Look for your booking
4. Check email for what happened

### Payment Failed?
1. Check card number, expiry, CVC
2. Ensure you have funds
3. Try a different card
4. Contact your bank if still failing

### Payment Charged But Booking Not Confirmed?
1. Check "My Bookings" status
2. If "Pending" → Instructor hasn't responded yet
3. If "Cancelled" → Instructor declined (you'll get refund)
4. Contact support if worried

### Haven't Received Email?
1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Make sure email address is correct
3. Wait 5 minutes (might be delayed)
4. Try refreshing the page
5. Contact support

### Instructor Not Responding?
1. Wait up to 24 hours
2. If no response → Booking auto-declines
3. You keep your payment + get refund
4. Book with different instructor

## FAQ by Role

### Student FAQ

**Q: Do I need my own car?**
A: Depends on instructor. Check their listing. Platform lessons usually use their car.

**Q: Can I book multiple instructors?**
A: Yes! Each instructor is independent.

**Q: What if I'm nervous?**
A: Many instructors specialize in nervous students. Filter by this or ask the instructor.

**Q: How long are lessons?**
A: Usually 1 or 2 hours. Check instructor's profile.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Depends on when you cancel. Before confirmation = yes. After = depends on policy.

**Q: How do I know the instructor is good?**
A: Check their star rating and read reviews from other students.

### Instructor FAQ

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Every Tuesday. Automatic transfer to your bank account.

**Q: How much commission do I pay?**
A: Depends on tier: Basic 15%, Pro 12%, Business 10%.

**Q: Can I turn off bookings temporarily?**
A: Yes! Go to Availability → Block Time for your vacation/illness.

**Q: What if a student cancels?**
A: You keep your earnings. No penalty.

**Q: How do I respond to negative reviews?**
A: Go to Profile → Reviews → Click review → Add response.

**Q: Can I change my hourly rate?**
A: Yes, anytime. Changes apply to new bookings only.

**Q: What if a student doesn't show up?**
A: Go to Bookings → Mark as "No-show" → You still get paid.
