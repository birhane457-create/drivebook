# Instructor Help Center — Getting Started

## Create Your Account & Get Approved

**What you need:**
- Email address
- Password (at least 8 characters)
- Full name
- Phone number
- Valid ID (driver's license or passport)
- ABN or business registration

**Steps:**

1. **Sign up:**
   - Go to DriveBook homepage
   - Click "Sign Up" (top right)
   - Select "I'm an Instructor"
   - Enter email and create password
   - Enter phone number and full name
   - Click "Create Account"

2. **Complete your profile:**
   - Log in to your dashboard
   - Click "Profile" → "Edit Profile"
   - Add:
     - Professional photo
     - Bio (what makes you a great instructor)
     - Years of experience
     - Teaching style
     - Specialties (nervous students, manual, etc.)

3. **Upload documents:**
   - Go to "Profile" → "Documents"
   - Upload:
     - ✅ Valid driver's license (front & back)
     - ✅ Valid working permit/visa (if not Australian)
     - ✅ Police check (if required)
     - ✅ Proof of ABN (if you have one)
   - All documents are reviewed by our admin team

4. **Approval process:**
   - Admin team reviews your profile (2-5 business days)
   - You'll get an email when approved
   - ⚠️ Until approved, you won't appear in student search
   - Once approved → Status changes to "Active"

**Tip:** Complete all documents immediately — don't wait. The sooner you upload, the sooner you can start earning.

## Set Your Availability

**Before students can book you, they need to see when you're available.**

**Steps:**

1. Go to dashboard → "Availability"
2. Click "Add Availability" or "Edit Schedule"
3. **For each day you teach:**
   - Select the day (Mon, Tue, Wed, etc.)
   - Click the times you're available
   - Example: 9 AM - 12 PM, 2 PM - 5 PM
   - Green = available, Gray = unavailable

4. **Set your recurring schedule:**
   - "Every Monday 9 AM - 5 PM"
   - "Tuesday & Thursday 10 AM - 3 PM"
   - "Weekends: Saturday 9 AM - 12 PM"

5. **Save changes**

**Important:**
- Only show times you're ACTUALLY available
- Students book these slots
- Don't set availability you can't keep
- Update availability if you take time off

**To block time (vacation, sick, etc.):**
1. Go to "Availability"
2. Click "Block Time"
3. Select date range
4. Save
5. Students can't book during blocked time

## Manage Your Pricing & Subscription Tier

**Subscription tiers affect your pricing and commission:**

| Tier | Monthly Fee | Commission | Perfect For |
|------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| **Basic** | $29 | 15% | Just starting out |
| **Pro** | $79 | 12% | Growing practice |
| **Business** | $199 | 10% | Full-time instructors |

**How commission works:**
- Student pays $100 for lesson
- You get $100 minus commission
- Example: Pro tier (12% commission) = You get $88

**Choose your tier:**
1. Go to "Subscription" in dashboard
2. See your current tier
3. Click "Upgrade" to higher tier (or "Downgrade")
4. Choose monthly or annual payment
5. Confirm and pay

**Benefits of higher tiers:**
- ✅ Lower commission (keep more money)
- ✅ More students see you (better ranking)
- ✅ Priority support
- ✅ Advanced analytics

**Tip:** Start with Basic tier, upgrade to Pro once you have 5+ regular students.

## Set Your Hourly Rate

**You control how much you charge students.**

**Steps:**
1. Go to "Settings" → "Pricing"
2. Set your base hourly rate
3. You can offer discounts for:
   - Package bookings (3+ lessons)
   - Regular weekly bookings
   - Longer sessions

4. Click "Save"

**Price guidance:**
- Experienced instructors (5+ years): $50-70/hour
- Mid-level (2-4 years): $35-50/hour
- Beginners (0-1 year): $25-35/hour
- Include pickup/drop-off in pricing

**Competition:**
- Check other instructors in your area
- Don't undercut too much (quality matters)
- Higher price = fewer students, but better quality students

## Accept & Manage Bookings

**Students book lessons, you decide to accept or decline.**

**View pending bookings:**
1. Go to dashboard → "Bookings"
2. See all bookings with status:
   - **Pending** — Waiting for you to accept
   - **Confirmed** — Accepted, lesson is scheduled
   - **Completed** — Lesson happened
   - **Cancelled** — Booking was cancelled

**Accept a booking:**
1. Click on the pending booking
2. Review details:
   - Student name and age
   - Date and time
   - Duration
   - Lesson type (Platform or Offline)
   - Student notes
3. Click "Accept Booking"
4. ✅ Student gets confirmation email

**Decline a booking:**
1. Click on the pending booking
2. Click "Decline"
3. (Optional) Add reason why
4. Student can book with another instructor

**Why decline?**
- Schedule conflict
- Unfamiliar with student's needs
- Too far away
- Fully booked

**Tip:** Accept or decline within 24 hours. Slow response rates hurt your ranking.

## Get Paid — Set Up Payouts

**Your earnings are automatically transferred to your bank account every week.**

**Set up payout:**
1. Go to "Settings" → "Payout & Tax Settings"
2. Select "Stripe Connect" (recommended)
3. Click "Connect with Stripe →"
4. You'll be taken to Stripe's secure page
5. Enter your:
   - Business type
   - Bank account details
   - Identity verification (driver's license)
6. ✅ Stripe verifies (usually 2-3 minutes)
7. Come back to DriveBook
8. Status shows "✅ Stripe account ready"

**How payouts work:**
- Earnings calculated every Tuesday
- Money transferred automatically to your bank
- Appears in account 1-2 business days
- You get an email receipt for each payout

**Tax details (Australia):**
- Add your ABN to reduce withholding tax (47% without ABN)
- Enter GST registration (if registered)
- These are optional but SAVE money

**Important:**
- Don't set up payout until you're fully approved (status = "Active")
- Use the same name on bank account as on your DriveBook profile
- Keep your Stripe account active (needed for payouts)

## Track Your Earnings

**See how much you're making:**

1. Go to dashboard → "Earnings"
2. See:
   - This week's earnings so far
   - This month's earnings
   - All-time total
   - Breakdown by student

**What's counted:**
- ✅ Completed lessons only
- ✅ Platform bookings (full amount minus commission)
- ✅ Offline bookings (if you recorded them)
- ❌ Cancelled lessons (not counted)
- ❌ Pending lessons (not counted yet)

**Earnings breakdown:**
- Lesson price (what student paid)
- Commission taken (platform fee)
- Your payout (what you receive)

**Example:**
- Student books 1-hour lesson at $50
- Pro tier commission: 12% = $6
- Your earnings: $44
- Platform keeps: $6

**View detailed history:**
1. Click "Transactions" → "View All"
2. See every transaction
3. Download receipt (for accounting)
4. Filter by date or status

- ✅ Quality of instruction

**Respond to reviews:**
- You can respond to negative reviews

- Show you care about improvement

## Offline Bookings (Manual Entry)

**Not all lessons go through the platform (e.g., cash payments, friends). You can record them manually.**

**Add an offline booking:**
1. Go to "Bookings" → "Add Offline Booking"
2. Enter:
   - Student name
   - Date and time
   - Duration (how long the lesson was)
   - Amount paid (how much you charged)
   - Notes (what you worked on)

3. Click "Save"

**Why record offline bookings?**
- Tracks your total earnings
- Counts toward your platform activity
- Helps with accounting

**Important:**
- Only record lessons you actually gave
- Be honest with amounts (for taxes)
- Offline bookings don't count toward commission (you keep 100%)

## Communication with Students

**Before and after lessons:**

**Message a student:**
1. Go to "Bookings"
2. Find the student's booking
3. Click "Message Student"
4. Type your message
5. Send

**What to message about:**
- Confirming details before lesson
- Asking if they have any questions
- Following up after lesson ("How'd it go?")
- Rescheduling if needed

**Keep it professional:**
- Use standard English
- Be friendly but professional
- Respond within a few hours

**Student messages you:**
- You'll get an email notification
- Check dashboard for messages
- Respond promptly (affects your rating)

## Common Questions

**Q: How long before I get approved?**
A: Usually 2-5 business days. Upload all documents immediately to speed it up.

**Q: Can I set different prices for different students?**
A: Not per student, but you can offer package discounts. Pricing applies to everyone equally.

**Q: What if a student cancels last-minute?**
A: You'll see cancellation in "Bookings". Your earnings stay the same (you're not charged).

**Q: How do I take time off?**
A: Go to "Availability" → "Block Time" → Select dates → Save.

**Q: Can I change my hourly rate?**
A: Yes! Go to "Settings" → "Pricing" anytime. Changes apply to new bookings only.

**Q: How do I get more bookings?**
A: Higher rating = higher search ranking. Focus on great lessons and quick responses.

**Q: What's the difference between Platform and Offline bookings?**
A: Platform = through DriveBook (tracked, commission taken). Offline = manual entry (you keep 100%).
