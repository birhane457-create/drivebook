# Student Help Center — Getting Started

## Create Your Account

**What you need:**
- Email address
- Password (at least 8 characters)
- Your full name
- Phone number

**Steps:**
1. Go to the DriveBook homepage
2. Click "Sign Up" (top right)
3. Select "I'm a Student" 
4. Enter your email address
5. Create a password (must include letters and numbers)
6. Enter your full name
7. Enter your phone number
8. Click "Create Account"
9. Check your email for a confirmation link and click it
10. ✅ Your account is ready!

**Tip:** You can add a profile photo and more details later from your dashboard.

---

## Find an Instructor

**What you're looking for:**
- Instructor's location or teaching style
- Availability that matches yours
- Reviews from other students
- Price (varies by instructor and package)

**Steps:**
1. Log in to your account
2. Click "Browse Instructors" or search from the home page
3. You'll see a list of instructors with:
   - Their name and photo
   - Star rating (based on student reviews)
   - Number of students they've taught
   - Their hourly rate
   - Short description of their teaching style

4. **Filter by:**
   - Location (city or suburb)
   - Price range
   - Availability
   - Specialties (e.g., "manual transmission", "nervous students")

5. Click on an instructor to see:
   - Full profile and qualifications
   - More detailed availability
   - Student reviews
   - What's included in their lessons

6. When you find someone you like, click "Book a Lesson"

## Book Your First Lesson

**What you need:**
- Choose an instructor (see "Find an Instructor" above)
- Pick a date and time that works for you
- Have enough wallet balance (or be ready to top up)

**Steps:**

1. **Click "Book a Lesson" on the instructor's profile**

2. **Select date and time:**
   - Calendar shows available slots (green = available, gray = booked)
   - Click on an available time
   - Choose lesson duration (usually 1 hour or 2 hours)

3. **Choose lesson type:**
   - **Platform Lesson** — Pick up from instructor's location or agree on a meeting spot
   - **Offline Lesson** — You arrange your own car/location

4. **Add any notes:**
   - Special requests (e.g., "nervous first-timer")
   - Areas you want to focus on
   - Any questions

5. **Review and check wallet:**
   - See the total price
   - Check if your wallet has enough balance
   - If short, you'll have 10 minutes to top up

6. **Confirm the booking:**
   - If wallet has enough: ✅ Booking confirmed immediately
   - If wallet is short: Email sent to top up within 10 minutes
   - **No instructor approval needed — booking is confirmed right away!**

**What happens next:**
- ✅ Booking Status = "Confirmed" (instantly after payment)
- ✅ You can see instructor details and meeting location
- ✅ You're all set — no waiting for instructor response
- ✅ Lesson is scheduled

**Important:** Booking is confirmed instantly after payment — the instructor doesn't need to accept it like in other apps. Your lesson is locked in!

## Reschedule or Cancel a Lesson

**Cancel before lesson confirmation:**
1. Go to "My Bookings" in your dashboard
2. Find the lesson you want to cancel
3. Click "Cancel Booking"
4. ✅ You'll get a refund (if cancelled before confirmation)

**Cancel after confirmation:**
1. Go to "My Bookings"
2. Find the confirmed lesson
3. Click "Reschedule" or "Cancel"
4. Follow the instructions
5. ⚠️ Cancellations within 24 hours may have penalties

**Reschedule to different time:**
1. Go to "My Bookings"
2. Click "Reschedule"
3. Pick a new date and time from available slots
4. Instructor will confirm the new time
5. Old booking is cancelled automatically

**Tip:** Try to reschedule as early as possible — instructors have limited availability.

## Make a Payment

**How payments work:**
- You top up your wallet with credit (via card)
- When you book, payment is taken from your wallet
- If wallet is short, booking is held for 10 minutes to top up

**Payment methods accepted:**
- Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Debit card
- Used to top up your wallet (one-time or recurring)

**How to pay:**

1. **Top up your wallet:**
   - Go to "Wallet" or "Payment Methods"
   - Click "Add Credit" or "Top Up"
   - Enter card details
   - Choose amount ($50, $100, etc.)
   - ✅ Credit added to wallet instantly

2. **When you book:**
   - System checks your wallet balance
   - **If enough balance:** ✅ Automatically deducted, booking confirmed
   - **If short:** Email sent with top-up link (10-minute window)

3. **Top up if needed:**
   - Click email link to add more credit
   - Enter card details
   - Balance updated instantly
   - Booking confirmed

**Refunds:**
- Cancel before confirmation → Wallet credit returned
- Cancel after confirmation → Subject to cancellation policy
- Instructor cancels → Full wallet credit returned
- Dispute a charge → Contact support

**Payment security:**
- Card details never stored on platform
- Stripe handles all payments (PCI compliant)
- Encrypted & secure

## Check Your Booking History

**View all your lessons:**
1. Log in to dashboard
2. Click "My Bookings"
3. See all lessons (past and future)

**Booking status meanings:**
- **Pending** — Waiting for instructor to accept
- **Confirmed** — Instructor accepted, lesson is scheduled
- **Completed** — Lesson finished, you can leave a review
- **Cancelled** — Booking was cancelled (no lesson)

**Find details:**
- Click on any booking to see:
  - Instructor name and contact
  - Date, time, and location
  - Duration and price
  - Any notes you added
  - Instructor's notes

## Leave a Review

**After your lesson:**
1. Go to "My Bookings"
2. Find the completed lesson
3. Click "Leave a Review"
4. Rate the instructor (1-5 stars)
5. Write what you thought:
   - What went well?
   - Areas for improvement?
   - Would you book again?
6. Click "Submit"

**Your review is:**
- ✅ Anonymous (hidden from instructor initially)
- ✅ Helpful for other students
- ✅ Part of instructor's rating

## Common Questions

**Q: How long until the instructor accepts my booking?**
A: Usually within 24 hours. You'll get an email confirmation when they accept.

**Q: Can I change the lesson date after booking?**
A: Yes! Click "Reschedule" in your booking (best done early).

**Q: What if I'm sick on lesson day?**
A: Cancel ASAP from "My Bookings". If cancelled before confirmation, you get a refund.

**Q: How do I know where to meet?**
A: Once the instructor accepts, check your booking details for the location and directions.

**Q: Can I book with a different instructor?**
A: Yes! Each instructor is independent. You can book with multiple instructors.

**Q: Do I need my own car?**
A: Depends on lesson type. Platform lessons usually use instructor's car. Check the listing.

## Need Help?

**Something not working?**
- Check your email for confirmation (sometimes goes to spam)

- Clear browser cache (Settings → Clear Browsing Data)

- Click the "?" Help icon on any page
- Go to "Support" → "Contact Us"
- Email support@drivebook.com.au

**Report a problem:**
- Click "Report Issue" in your booking
- Include details of what went wrong
- Support team will respond within 24 hours
