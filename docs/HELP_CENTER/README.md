## Common Questions

### Students

**Q: Why is my booking still "Pending"?**
A: The instructor hasn't responded yet. They have 24 hours to accept. Check your email for updates.

**Q: Can I change my lesson date?**
A: Yes! Click "Reschedule" in your booking. The instructor will confirm the new time.

**Q: Do I get a refund if I cancel?**
A: Depends on when. Cancelled before confirmation = full refund. After confirmation = subject to instructor's policy.

**Q: How do I know where to meet?**
A: Once the instructor accepts, your booking shows the meeting location and directions.

---

### Instructors

**Q: How do I get approved faster?**
A: Upload all required documents immediately. Admin reviews within 2-5 business days.

**Q: Can I have custom pricing for different students?**
A: Not individually, but you can offer package discounts for multiple bookings.

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Earnings are calculated every Tuesday and transferred to your bank account automatically.

**Q: What if a student cancels?**
A: You keep your earnings. The cancellation fee (if any) goes back to the student or platform.

**Q: How can I get more bookings?**
A: Maintain a high rating (4.5+ stars). Respond to bookings quickly. Be professional and provide great lessons.

## Account & Privacy

### Change Password
1. Go to Profile → Settings
2. Click "Change Password"
3. Enter old password and new password
4. Click "Save"

### Update Profile Information
1. Go to Profile → Settings
2. Edit your information
3. Click "Save Changes"

### Delete My Account
1. Go to Profile → Settings
2. Scroll to "Danger Zone"
3. Click "Delete Account"
4. ⚠️ This is permanent and cannot be undone

### Privacy & Notifications
1. Go to Settings → Privacy
2. Control who can see your profile
3. Manage email notifications
4. Set communication preferences

## Troubleshooting

### Page Won't Load
- Refresh the page (F5 or Cmd+R)
- Clear browser cache:
  - Chrome: Settings → Clear Browsing Data
  - Safari: Develop → Empty Caches
  - Firefox: History → Clear Recent History

### Can't Log In
- Check your email is correct
- Reset password if forgotten:
  - Click "Forgot Password" on login page
  - Enter email
  - Check email for reset link
  - Create new password

### Payment Failed
- Check your card details
- Ensure card hasn't expired
- Try a different payment method
- Contact your bank if still failing

### Booking Disappeared
- Check "My Bookings" → "Cancelled" tab
- The instructor may have declined
- Check your email for notification

### Not Getting Email Notifications
- Check spam/junk folder
- Go to Settings → Notifications
- Ensure notifications are turned on
- Add support@drivebook.com.au to contacts

- Students: support@drivebook.com.au

**Response time:**
- Most questions answered within 24 hours
- Urgent issues (payment problems) prioritized

**What to include when reporting an issue:**
- What were you trying to do?
- What happened instead?
- Screenshot (if possible)
- Your browser/device
- Date and time of the problem
