Taking time off as a driving instructor is simple — but only if your booking page reflects it accurately. If you go on holiday without updating your availability, students may book slots you can't fulfil, leading to cancellations and a poor first impression.

Here's how to manage holidays and time off cleanly in DriveBook.

## Option 1: Block Specific Dates

The most targeted approach is to block individual dates or a date range directly in your availability calendar.

In your DriveBook dashboard, go to Availability → Block Dates. Select the dates you'll be away and save. Those dates immediately become unavailable on your booking page — students cannot book slots during that period.

This is the best option for a standard holiday or planned absence. It leaves your regular weekly availability intact once the blocked period ends.

## Option 2: Temporarily Disable Availability

If you're taking an extended break or an indefinite break, you can temporarily disable your profile. This prevents any new bookings from being made without permanently altering your availability settings.

Go to Settings → Profile → Availability and toggle your profile to unavailable. Your booking page will show that you're currently not accepting bookings. Re-enable it when you return.

## Option 3: Adjust Weekly Availability

If your schedule changes long-term — for example, you're returning to part-time teaching after a break — update your weekly availability template. This sets new regular hours going forward without needing to block dates individually.

Go to Availability → Weekly Schedule and update your available days and times.

## Existing Bookings During Your Break

Blocking your calendar from new bookings doesn't automatically cancel existing bookings. If you have lessons already confirmed during your planned leave period, you'll need to:

1. Contact those students directly to reschedule
2. Update the lesson dates in your dashboard

It's good practice to block your holiday dates well in advance — before students have a chance to book the affected slots — rather than cancelling confirmed bookings after the fact.

## Communicating Your Absence

Students who visit your booking page during your blocked period will see that no slots are available. For regular students you have an ongoing relationship with, a brief message is courteous: "I'm on leave from [date] to [date] and will be back taking bookings from [date]."

A short note in your DriveBook bio during the leave period can also help set expectations for anyone who visits your profile.

## When You Return

Re-enable your availability and check your calendar to make sure your settings are correct. Students who tried to book while you were away may visit again — a complete, updated profile with fresh availability is what converts them.
