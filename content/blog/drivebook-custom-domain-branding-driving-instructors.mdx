When a prospective student searches for a driving instructor in your suburb, they often visit the booking page before they call. What they see on that page — your name, your photo, your logo, your colours — determines whether they book with you or keep searching.

DriveBook's branding and custom domain features let you create a professional, personalised booking experience without hiring a developer or maintaining a separate website.

## Your Personal Booking Page

Every instructor on DriveBook gets their own booking page at a subdomain address — for example, `john.drivebook.com.au`. This page shows:

- Your profile photo and car photo
- Your bio, vehicle type, and languages
- Your pricing, including packages and PDA test packs
- Your service areas and availability
- Your student reviews
- A booking form that works directly

When you share this link on social media, put it in your email signature, or print it on business cards, students land on a page that looks like yours — not a generic directory listing.

## Custom Branding (PRO and above)

On PRO, Studio, and Business plans, you can apply your own branding to your booking page:

- Upload your logo
- Set your brand colours (used in CTAs, accents, and highlights throughout the page)
- Your name or school name appears in the navigation instead of "DriveBook"

The result is a booking experience that looks and feels like your own website — while DriveBook handles all the booking infrastructure behind it. Your page URL stays on `yourname.drivebook.com.au` at this tier.

## Custom Domain (Studio and Business)

Studio and Business tier instructors can connect their own domain to DriveBook. If you own `johnsdrivingschool.com.au`, you can point it to DriveBook and all the booking, payment, and confirmation infrastructure runs through your domain.

Students visit `johnsdrivingschool.com.au`, they see your branding and booking form, they book and pay — and everything is powered by DriveBook without them knowing.

This is the experience of running a professional driving school website, without the development cost or ongoing maintenance.

## Why This Matters

Branding builds trust. A student who lands on a page that shows your logo, your colours, and your photos feels more confident booking with you than a student who lands on a generic listing.

It also makes you easier to refer. When a student passes their test and tells their friends "book through my instructor's website," they're sending them directly to your booking page — not to a directory where you compete with other instructors.

## Setting It Up

Custom branding can be configured in your DriveBook dashboard under Branding:
1. Upload your logo
2. Set your primary and secondary colours
3. Preview your page
4. Activate

Custom domain setup requires pointing your domain's DNS records to DriveBook — the dashboard walks you through the steps, and our support team can assist.
