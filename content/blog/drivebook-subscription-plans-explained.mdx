DriveBook offers four subscription plans designed for different stages of a driving instruction business — from a solo instructor just starting out to a driving school managing ten or more instructors. Understanding what each plan includes helps you choose the right level from day one and know when it makes sense to move up.

## The Four Plans at a Glance

**BASIC** — For solo instructors getting started  
**PRO** — For established solo instructors wanting more capability  
**STUDIO** — For small driving schools with a handful of instructors  
**BUSINESS** — For larger driving schools and multi-location operations

Each plan is available monthly or annually. Annual billing is discounted compared to paying month by month.

## BASIC: The Starting Point

BASIC is designed for instructors who are new to DriveBook or running a straightforward solo operation. It includes:

- Your public booking profile and subdomain
- Online booking and calendar management
- Automated SMS and email reminders
- Student wallet payments
- Basic lesson history and student records

BASIC gives you everything you need to replace a paper diary, take online bookings, and collect payments professionally. For a new instructor building their first client base, it's the right place to start.

The limitation at BASIC is the cap on active students and bookings per month, and the absence of some advanced features like AI receptionist access, custom domain, and detailed analytics.

## PRO: The Established Solo Instructor

PRO is where most active solo instructors land once they're running a full or near-full schedule. It adds:

- Higher student and booking limits
- Custom domain support (your own domain pointing to your DriveBook booking page)
- AI phone receptionist (24/7 automated call answering and booking)
- Detailed revenue and booking analytics
- Priority support

The custom domain and AI receptionist are the features that move the needle most at PRO. The custom domain gives you the professional branding of a proper business website without building one. The AI receptionist captures bookings from calls you can't answer during lessons — typically one of the largest sources of lost revenue for solo instructors.

## STUDIO: The Small Driving School

STUDIO is designed for driving schools with two to six instructors operating under a shared brand. It adds:

- Multi-instructor management from one admin dashboard
- Per-instructor calendar and availability management
- School-level branding applied across all instructor profiles
- Instructor performance reporting (bookings, completion rates, earnings)
- Centralised student management

At this tier, the admin dashboard shifts from personal tool to business management tool. A school owner can see what every instructor has booked, track cancellation rates, and manage payouts centrally.

## BUSINESS: The Larger Operation

BUSINESS is for schools with more than six instructors or multi-location operations requiring the full platform capability:

- Unlimited instructors
- Advanced reporting and revenue dashboards
- Staff governance and role-based access
- Priority onboarding and dedicated support

For a large school, the staff governance and role-based access features are particularly important — different staff members need different levels of visibility and control, and BUSINESS makes that manageable.

## Starting on a Trial

New instructors start on a free trial period, which gives access to features at the PRO tier for a limited time. This lets you evaluate the platform properly before committing to a paid plan.

When the trial ends, you choose the plan that fits your current operation. There's no penalty for starting on BASIC and upgrading later as your business grows.

## Choosing the Right Plan

The simplest rule: start on the plan that matches where you are now, not where you hope to be.

A new instructor booking two or three lessons a week doesn't need BUSINESS tier features. Starting on BASIC keeps costs low while you're building. As your schedule fills and the AI receptionist and custom domain start to look useful, that's when PRO makes sense.

For anyone managing multiple instructors, STUDIO is the right starting point — the per-instructor management tools alone are worth the upgrade from solo-instructor plans.

## Moving Between Plans

You can upgrade at any time and the change takes effect immediately. Downgrading is available too, subject to billing cycle timing.

There are no lock-in contracts on monthly billing. Annual billing comes with the discount and runs for the year.
