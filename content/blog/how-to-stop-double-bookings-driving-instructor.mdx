A double booking is one of the most embarrassing situations a driving instructor can face. You arrive to collect a student and realise you already have another student in the car — or you get two confirmation messages for the same time slot and have to call one student to cancel.

It damages trust, costs you refunds, and can lose you both students.

## Why Double Bookings Happen

Double bookings almost always come from fragmented systems. The most common causes:

**Multiple booking channels** — You take bookings by phone, by text, by Facebook message, and from an online form. Each channel is a separate place to track — and when you're busy, slots get added to one channel but not the others.

**Manual calendar management** — A whiteboard or paper diary requires you to manually cross-reference every new booking. One missed check creates a conflict.

**Delayed updates** — Someone books you at 9pm via a form. You check your phone in the morning. In the meantime, another student texted and you confirmed verbally. Same slot, two students.

**Multiple devices** — If you're updating a calendar on your phone but someone at home books you through a family member who checks a different calendar, conflicts arise.

## The Only Reliable Solution

The only way to eliminate double bookings permanently is to have a single source of truth for your availability — one calendar that all bookings flow into and that blocks slots the moment they're taken.

When a student books through your DriveBook page, the slot is locked immediately in your calendar. No one else can book that time. It doesn't matter whether the booking came through the website, the AI phone receptionist, or you entering it manually — it all updates the same calendar.

This works because every booking path (online, phone, manual entry) writes to the same system. There is no second calendar to check.

## What to Do With Non-Platform Bookings

If a student contacts you through a channel outside DriveBook — a text message, a social media DM — you can record that booking manually in your dashboard. The slot is then blocked in your calendar, preventing any new booking from being accepted for that time.

This is important: the system only prevents double bookings from platform channels by default. If you take a booking outside the platform and don't record it, the system doesn't know. Build the habit of entering all bookings immediately.

## Setting Your Availability Correctly

Double bookings also happen when your availability settings don't reflect reality. If your calendar shows you as open on Wednesday afternoons but you've committed to a regular pickup at that time, every Wednesday is a potential double booking.

Review your availability settings regularly — especially after you add regular students, after school holidays, or when your personal schedule changes.

## Buffer Times

Some instructors set buffer times between lessons to account for travel, fuel, or extended lessons that run over. Without buffers, a lesson that finishes late creates a conflict with the next student's pickup time — which while not technically a double booking, creates the same problem.

DriveBook lets you set lesson duration and buffer time in your availability settings.

## The Result

Instructors who move all booking channels through a single system report that double bookings essentially disappear. The discipline of recording every booking in one place, combined with automatic slot-locking for online bookings, eliminates the fragmentation that causes conflicts.
