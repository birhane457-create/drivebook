Most people underestimate what it costs to get a driver's licence. They think about lesson fees — but there are other costs that add up: the licence application, the theory test, the PDA fee, and the actual licence card.

This guide breaks down every cost involved in getting your driver's licence in Perth in 2025, and shows you where you can reduce the total without cutting corners on preparation.

## The Full Cost Breakdown

### 1. Learner's Permit (L plates)

Before you can take lessons or drive on the road, you need to obtain your learner's permit from the Department of Transport WA.

The process involves:
- A theory test (knowledge test) at a DoT licensing centre
- **Cost:** Approximately $34–$38 for the learner's permit licence card fee (fees updated periodically — check the DoT WA website for current rates)

The theory test is free to sit, but you may need to pay for study materials or apps. There are free practice tests available on the DoT WA website.

### 2. Driving Lessons

This is the biggest variable in the total cost.

**Perth instructor rates in 2025:** typically $65–$95 per hour, depending on the instructor's experience, vehicle type, and your location.

**How many lessons you need** depends on how much supervised driving you do outside of professional lessons. Students who only rely on paid lessons and do no practice between them will need significantly more hours.

A rough estimate for a first-time learner with regular supervised practice between lessons:

| Student profile | Approx lessons needed | Approx cost |
|---|---|---|
| Frequent supervised practice (3+ hrs/week with family) | 15–20 hrs | $1,050–$1,900 |
| Occasional supervised practice | 25–35 hrs | $1,750–$3,325 |
| No supervised practice, lessons only | 35–50+ hrs | $2,450–$4,750+ |

These are estimates — actual numbers vary significantly by individual.

### 3. Lesson Packages vs Pay-As-You-Go

Most Perth instructors offer packages of 5, 10, or 15+ hours at a discounted rate. Typical savings:

- 5-hour package: ~5% discount
- 10-hour package: ~8–10% discount
- 15-hour package: ~10–12% discount

On a 20-hour programme at $80/hr, a 10% package discount saves you $160. That's worth taking if you're committed to seeing the lessons through.

**One caution:** ask about the refund policy for unused lessons before purchasing a package. Reputable instructors will refund unused hours if circumstances change.

### 4. The PDA Fee

The Practical Driving Assessment is booked through the DoT WA. The current fee is approximately **$59–$75** per attempt (check DoT WA for current rates — they are updated periodically).

If you fail and need to rebook, you pay the fee again. This is another reason to prepare properly before your first attempt.

### 5. The Driver's Licence

Once you pass the PDA and hold your P plates for the required period, your full licence application costs approximately $70–$100 for the first licence period (3–5 years depending on the class you choose).

## Total Estimated Cost

For a first-time learner in Perth who practises regularly with a supervisor between lessons:

| Item | Estimated cost |
|---|---|
| Learner's permit | ~$35 |
| Driving lessons (20 hrs at $80/hr, with package discount) | ~$1,440 |
| PDA fee (first attempt) | ~$65 |
| P-plate licence | ~$85 |
| **Total** | **~$1,625** |

For someone with no supervised practice who needs 40+ hours of professional lessons, the total could easily exceed $3,500.

## How to Reduce the Total Cost

### Drive regularly with a family member or supervisor

WA requires a minimum number of supervised logbook hours before you can sit the PDA, including a required proportion with a qualified instructor. Licensing requirements may change — always check the [Department of Transport WA website](https://www.transport.wa.gov.au) for the current requirements before you start.

The key point: every hour you log with a supervisor is one fewer paid lesson you need.

Even 2 hours of supervised driving per week alongside your professional lessons will significantly reduce how many lessons you need and how long you spend as a learner.

### Book a package through a verified instructor

Package pricing is cheaper than pay-as-you-go for the same hours. Just confirm the refund policy before committing.

### Pass the PDA the first time

At ~$65 per attempt, failing and rebooking adds cost. More importantly, you'll need additional lessons to address the issues that caused the failure. Investing in proper PDA preparation — including a mock test with your instructor — is cheaper than a second attempt.

### Don't rush

Rushing to sit the PDA before you're ready is the most expensive decision you can make. An extra 3-4 lessons that get you properly prepared cost less than a failed PDA plus the lessons to fix the gaps and rebook.
