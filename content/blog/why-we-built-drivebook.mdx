Driving instructors are small business owners. They manage their own schedules, handle their own marketing, collect their own payments, maintain their own vehicles, and build their own student relationships — all while spending most of their day in a moving car.

Despite this, the tools most of them used until recently looked like this: a paper diary or a basic calendar app, payments collected in cash or by bank transfer, student records kept in memory or a notebook, bookings managed through phone calls and text messages.

It wasn't that instructors hadn't thought about doing things better. It was that no one had built the right tool for them.

## The Problem We Set Out to Solve

The core problem was fragmentation. Every part of the instructor's workflow — booking, payment, reminders, student records, income tracking — existed in a separate place, connected by manual effort.

When a new student called, the instructor had to answer (if they could), agree a time, write it down, collect payment later, remember to send a reminder, and add the income to a spreadsheet. Each step was its own task.

The result was a significant amount of time spent on administrative overhead that added no value to the student relationship and no value to the teaching itself.

We believed that time should be spent teaching, not managing logistics.

## What We Built

DriveBook brings the whole workflow into one connected platform:

A student finds an instructor, sees real availability, books and pays online. The booking confirms automatically. A reminder goes out automatically. The instructor sees the lesson in their dashboard without doing anything. The income is recorded. The student's record is created.

None of those steps require the instructor to take action. They happen because the system is connected.

For instructors who are busy — which is all of them — the difference between a connected system and a fragmented one is hours per week.

## The AI Receptionist

One of our most distinctive features is the AI phone receptionist — a 24/7 answering service that checks the instructor's calendar in real time and confirms bookings over the phone.

We built this because missed calls are one of the most consistent sources of lost revenue for driving instructors. An instructor in a car cannot answer the phone. A student who doesn't reach anyone often doesn't call back.

The AI receptionist answers immediately, handles the booking conversation, and creates the confirmed lesson without the instructor doing anything. Students who would otherwise have moved on to the next instructor in search results instead get a confirmed lesson.

## The Branding Vision

We believe every driving instructor should have the tools to present as a professional business, not just a phone number on a flyer.

That means a dedicated booking page at their own subdomain. A custom domain option for those who want to fully own their online presence. Logo and brand colour support. A public profile that's indexed by Google and accumulates genuine student reviews over time.

An instructor using DriveBook should look as professional as any well-run service business in any other industry.

## Multi-Instructor Schools

As we've worked with more users, we've built out features for driving schools with multiple instructors — school-level branding, individual instructor calendars with central admin visibility, per-instructor earnings and performance reporting, and coordinated student assignment.

The aim is the same: reduce the management overhead so school owners can focus on maintaining quality and growing their business rather than coordinating schedules via group chat.

## Where We're Going

We're a young platform in a market that has historically been underserved by technology. There's significant room to build.

The features we're working toward — better analytics, improved AI capabilities, stronger SEO tools for instructors, and better tools for driving school owners — are all oriented around the same goal we started with: give driving instructors the tools to run professional businesses, spend less time on admin, and focus on what they actually do well.
