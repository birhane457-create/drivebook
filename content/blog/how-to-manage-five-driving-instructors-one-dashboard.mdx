Managing one driving instructor is simple. Managing five is a different operation entirely.

Five separate calendars. Five sets of student relationships. Five income streams to track. Five sets of availability to maintain. Enquiries that need to be routed to the right instructor. Revenue that needs to be reconciled and split correctly.

Most small driving schools try to manage this through a combination of group chats, shared spreadsheets, and phone calls. It works — until it doesn't. The cracks show up as double bookings, miscommunication, inconsistent student experiences, and an owner spending more time on coordination than on anything else.

## The Central Dashboard Approach

A multi-instructor dashboard gives the school owner — or admin — a single view across all instructors. From one screen, you can see:

- Which instructors have availability this week
- Which students are assigned to which instructor
- Revenue broken down by instructor
- Upcoming lessons across the whole team
- Pending bookings that need assignment

This replaces the back-and-forth of messaging each instructor to check their diary.

## Assigning Students to Instructors

When a new student enquires, the admin can see at a glance which instructor has availability in the student's area. They assign the student directly — no group chat, no "who's free Friday morning" messages.

The assigned instructor sees the new booking in their own dashboard. The student receives a confirmation with their instructor's details.

Students can also be matched by preference — language, vehicle type, availability. If a student specifically needs a female instructor or someone who teaches manual cars in a specific suburb, the admin can filter by those criteria immediately.

## Each Instructor Has Their Own View

Instructors on the team don't see each other's student records or income. They see their own calendar, their own students, their own earnings. The admin sees everything.

This separation matters for privacy (students' personal details stay with their assigned instructor) and for practical reasons (instructors don't need to see data that's irrelevant to their work).

## Revenue Tracking Per Instructor

A single bank account receiving payments from multiple instructors' students is easy to mix up. A dashboard that attributes each payment to the correct instructor, and generates per-instructor income reports, replaces the manual work of reconciling who earned what.

At payout time, the school owner can see exactly what each instructor is owed. Payouts can be calculated automatically based on your commission or fee structure.

## Consistent Student Experience

One of the risks in a multi-instructor school is that the student experience varies. One instructor sends reminders. Another doesn't. One uses a booking page. Another takes cash in the car.

When all instructors use the same platform, the student experience is consistent regardless of which instructor they're assigned to. Every student gets a confirmation SMS, a reminder, and a clear payment process — automatically.
