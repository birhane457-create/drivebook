The best driving instructors don't just teach — they track. After each lesson, they know where each student stands, what to focus on next, and how to structure the next session to build on what came before.

For most instructors, this tracking happens in memory or on paper. DriveBook replaces that with a structured feedback and progress system built into the platform.

## How Lesson Feedback Works in DriveBook

After each lesson, you log feedback directly in your DriveBook dashboard. This takes about two minutes and covers:

- Skills worked on during the session
- What the student did well
- What to focus on in the next lesson
- An overall performance rating

The feedback is recorded against the student's profile. The student can see it in their client dashboard — they log in and see a summary of every lesson, their progress over time, and what their instructor has recommended they practise.

## Why Students Value It

Most learner drivers have no idea where they stand in their preparation for the PDA. They feel like each lesson is just driving around without a clear trajectory. Seeing structured feedback changes that.

A student who can log in and read "your roundabout entries have improved significantly — focus on mirror checks on open roads before our next lesson" has a much clearer sense of purpose going into the next session. They're more likely to practise, more motivated to book the next lesson, and more likely to pass their test when they sit it.

This builds loyalty. Students who see their progress tend to complete their full lesson programme with you instead of drifting away mid-course.

## PDA Preparation Tracking

DriveBook's feedback system is aligned with WA PDA assessment criteria. You can log performance against the specific skill categories the examiner assesses — observation, control, positioning, decision-making, communication.

Over time, the student's profile shows which areas are strong and which need more work. When you're approaching test readiness, you can point to the feedback history and say confidently: "You're consistently passing on these categories — let's make sure these others are solid before we book the test."

## For the Instructor

Beyond the student benefit, the feedback history is useful for you too:

- When a student hasn't had a lesson in a few weeks, you can refresh yourself on where you left off without relying on memory
- If a student changes instructors, the incoming instructor can see the history
- If a student disputes feedback or progress, the records are documented

## Setting It Up

Lesson feedback is available to all DriveBook instructors. After each lesson, open the booking in your dashboard and tap "Add feedback." It takes two minutes and costs nothing extra.
