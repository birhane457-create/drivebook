A no-show is one of the most frustrating things that can happen to a driving instructor. You've blocked the time, driven to the pickup address, and waited. Nothing. The student doesn't answer their phone. The hour is gone.

For an instructor taking 25 lessons per week, even one no-show per week represents around 4% of total income simply evaporating.

## Why No-Shows Happen

Most no-shows are not deliberate. Students — particularly younger learner drivers — genuinely forget. A lesson booked two weeks ago drifts out of memory. Life gets busy. The notification that might have reminded them never came.

The fix is almost always the same: a timely reminder.

## Why SMS Works Better Than Email

Email reminders work, but SMS has a much higher open rate. Most people read an SMS within minutes of receiving it. An email might sit unread for hours or get lost in a promotional folder.

For driving lessons — where the reminder needs to land the day before or the morning of — SMS is the right channel. Students see it, respond to it, and if they need to cancel, they'll usually do so in time for you to potentially fill the slot.

## The Timing That Works

A two-touch reminder system works well for most instructors:

**24 hours before** — A reminder that the lesson is tomorrow. This is the most important touch. Students who have genuinely forgotten have time to reschedule, and you have time to fill the slot if they cancel.

**2 hours before** — A final confirmation. By this point cancellations are unlikely to be filled, but the reminder reduces the chance of a student simply losing track of the time.

Some instructors also send a booking confirmation SMS at the time of booking. This sets the expectation immediately and plants the lesson in the student's memory.

## What the Message Should Say

Keep it brief and clear:

*"Hi [Name], reminder: driving lesson tomorrow [Day] at [Time], pickup at [Address]. Reply CANCEL to cancel. — [Instructor Name]"*

Including the pickup address is important. Students sometimes book with two or three instructors or forget which instructor they booked with. Specifics help.

## What Happens to Cancellations You Do Receive

When a student cancels 24 hours out (prompted by the reminder), you have a window to offer the slot to other students. If you have a waitlist or students looking for short-notice slots, you can fill it.

Without a reminder, that cancellation often comes at 6am on the day, too late to reschedule.

## The Net Effect

Instructors who use automated SMS reminders typically see a noticeable drop in last-minute cancellations and no-shows. The cost of sending an automated SMS is a fraction of the lesson rate. The return — in lessons that go ahead instead of being lost — is significant.

It's one of the lowest-effort, highest-return improvements available to any driving instructor.
