When people hear "AI receptionist," they often picture a phone tree — press 1 for bookings, press 2 for cancellations. DriveBook's AI is different. It handles a full natural-language conversation, the same way a human receptionist would.

Here's an honest explanation of how it works, what it can do, and where its limits are.

## How AI Voice Booking Actually Works

Modern voice AI uses large language models to understand spoken language and generate appropriate responses. The AI doesn't follow a rigid script — it understands intent.

When a student says "I want to book a lesson" or "Can I move my appointment?" or "How much do you charge?", the AI understands what they're asking regardless of exactly how they phrase it.

DriveBook's AI receptionist is connected to your live booking system. This means:

- It reads your actual availability (not a cached copy)
- It uses your current rates (if you update your price, the AI quotes the new price immediately)
- Bookings it creates appear in your dashboard in real time
- When payment is confirmed via Stripe, the booking is locked and the student gets an SMS

## What the AI Handles

**New bookings:**
The AI identifies the student's location, finds available instructors nearby (or the specific instructor if they have a dedicated number), presents available slots, takes student details, reads everything back for confirmation, then creates the booking and sends a payment link.

**Cancellations:**
The AI looks up the booking by phone number, checks the instructor's refund policy, tells the student exactly what refund they'll receive, sends an SMS verification code to confirm identity, gets verbal confirmation, then cancels and processes the refund.

**Reschedules:**
Identity verification, slot search, read-back of new time, verbal confirmation, booking updated, updated SMS confirmation sent.

**Returning callers:**
If a student's call drops mid-booking and they call back, the AI recognises them: *"Welcome back — I can see you were booking a lesson about 4 minutes ago. I've resent your payment link."*

## Where the AI Has Limits

The AI is designed to handle bookings, cancellations, and reschedules. It is not designed for:

- Complex complaints or disputes
- Questions that require instructor judgment (e.g., "Am I ready for my test?")
- Anything outside its defined scope

For questions it can't answer, the AI tells the caller to message the instructor directly. It doesn't make up answers or pretend to know things it doesn't.

## Why Driving School AI Is Harder Than It Looks

Booking a driving lesson sounds simple, but there are real edge cases:

**Short-notice bookings** — if a student tries to book a lesson starting in two hours, auto-confirming could put an instructor in an impossible position. DriveBook's AI flags short-notice requests as pending and notifies the instructor to approve or decline.

**Duplicate bookings** — before confirming, the AI checks whether the caller already has a booking with the same instructor on the same day. If so, it asks whether they meant to add another lesson or change the existing one.

**Payment timing** — after a payment link is sent, the AI doesn't guess whether the student paid. It polls the payment status in real time (up to three checks) and only confirms the booking when Stripe confirms the payment.

These aren't features most general-purpose AI assistants handle correctly for the driving instruction context. DriveBook's AI is trained specifically for this use case.
