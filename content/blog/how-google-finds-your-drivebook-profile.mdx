When someone searches "driving instructor Balcatta" or "driving lessons Midland WA," Google returns a mix of results: map listings, websites, and directory pages. Your DriveBook profile can appear in those results — but only if it's set up to be found.

Here's how Google discovers and ranks your profile, and what you can do to improve your visibility.

## Your Booking Page Is Publicly Indexed

Every instructor's DriveBook booking page is a public webpage with its own URL (`yourname.drivebook.com.au`). Google's web crawlers visit and index these pages, which means your profile can appear in search results.

This is different from a Facebook profile or a directory listing — those are indexed on the parent platform's domain. Your DriveBook page is your own URL, which has long-term SEO value as it ages and accumulates reviews.

## What Google Looks For

Google's local search algorithm considers several factors when deciding which instructor profiles to surface:

**Relevance** — Does your profile mention the suburbs you operate in? Do your service area settings and bio text include suburb names that match what a student is searching?

**Reviews** — The number and recency of reviews is one of the strongest signals Google uses for local business ranking. An instructor with 30 genuine reviews ranks higher than one with none, all else being equal.

**Completeness** — A complete profile (photo, bio, service areas, pricing, vehicle types, languages) gives Google more signals to work with than a sparse one. Incomplete profiles are harder to classify and rank accurately.

**Freshness** — Recently updated profiles and recently received reviews signal an active business. A profile that was last updated two years ago looks dormant.

## Your Google Business Profile

Separate from your DriveBook page, you should also set up a Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business). This is the listing that appears in the map results — the "Local Pack" — for searches like "driving instructor near me."

Your Google Business Profile and your DriveBook booking page work together:
- GBP surfaces your business in map results
- DriveBook booking page provides the destination for students who click through

In your GBP, link your DriveBook booking URL as your website address. This connects the two and gives Google a clear picture of your business.

## Reviews on Both Platforms

Ideally, you collect reviews on both your DriveBook profile and your Google Business Profile. Google reviews directly influence your map ranking. DriveBook reviews appear on your booking page and influence booking conversions.

After each student passes their test, ask them to leave a review on both. Keep it easy — send direct links to both review pages.

## How to Optimise Your Profile for Search

1. **Use suburb names naturally in your bio** — "I teach in Morley, Bassendean, and Bayswater" is more useful than "I teach across Perth's northern suburbs." Specific names match specific searches.

2. **Set your service suburbs accurately** — The suburb data from your availability settings is used in DriveBook's own search and signals your coverage area to search engines.

3. **Collect reviews consistently** — A steady trickle of reviews over time is more valuable than a burst. Ask every student who passes.

4. **Keep your profile updated** — When you add a new service area, update your pricing, or change your vehicle, update your profile. Activity signals relevance.

5. **Consider a custom domain** — A custom domain (`yourname.com.au`) pointing to your booking page adds another SEO layer. It's your own domain aging and accumulating authority separately from the DriveBook subdomain.
