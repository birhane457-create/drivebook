Driving instructors are in the car 5–7 hours a day. During those hours, they cannot safely answer the phone. This creates a structural problem that costs the average instructor thousands of dollars a week.

## Why Instructors Miss Calls

The maths are simple. If you teach from 8am to 5pm with one break, you're unavailable for phone calls for roughly 80% of your working day. Add evenings and weekends — when students typically search and call — and the gap is even larger.

Most instructors rely on one of these workarounds:

- **Voicemail** — students rarely leave voicemails. Research consistently shows that callers who reach voicemail move on rather than waiting for a callback.
- **Callback policy** — "I'll call you back within 24 hours." Students looking to book a lesson are not planning 24 hours ahead. They're ready now.
- **Teaching gaps** — scheduling admin time between lessons to return calls. This works, but it doesn't address evening and weekend enquiries, and it's time you're not earning.

## What Missed Calls Actually Cost

The average DriveBook lesson package is $710. A realistic estimate for a full-time instructor is 3–5 missed calls per week that could convert to bookings.

At a conservative 40% conversion rate:

- 3 missed calls × 40% × $710 = **$852/week in missed revenue**
- 5 missed calls × 40% × $710 = **$1,420/week in missed revenue**

Over a year, that's $44,000–$73,000 in revenue that either went to a competitor or simply didn't happen.

These numbers are rough estimates — your conversion rate and call volume will vary. But the directional point holds: missed calls are not a minor inconvenience for a driving instructor, they're a material business cost.

## The Sunday Evening Problem

Sunday evening is peak search time for driving lesson bookings. Students have the week ahead in mind, they've just thought about the test they need to sit, they're motivated to act.

If your phone goes to voicemail at 8pm on Sunday, that enquiry is lost. An AI receptionist that answers at 8pm on Sunday — with your real availability, your real prices, your professional greeting — converts that enquiry into a confirmed booking before the student has time to reconsider.
