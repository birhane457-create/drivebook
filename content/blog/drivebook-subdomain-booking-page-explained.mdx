When you join DriveBook, you get a public booking page — your own corner of the internet where students can learn about you, see your pricing, read your reviews, and book a lesson without calling.

This page sits at a subdomain address: `yourname.drivebook.com.au` (or your custom domain if you're on Studio or Business plan).

## What Your Booking Page Shows

**Your profile** — Name, photo, and car photo. First impressions matter. Instructors with professional photos convert significantly better than those with no photo at all.

**Your bio** — A short description about your teaching style, experience, and what students can expect. You write this in your dashboard settings.

**Pricing** — Your hourly rate, available lesson packages, and PDA test pack pricing. All displayed clearly so students know what they're committing to before they start the booking form.

**Availability** — The next available lesson slots, based on your real-time calendar.

**Reviews** — Genuine reviews from previous students. New students read these before deciding to book.

**Languages and vehicle types** — Shown prominently so students looking for a manual car or a teacher who speaks Arabic can identify you immediately.

**Booking form** — Students enter their pickup address, choose a package, and pay — all on the same page.

## How Students Reach Your Page

**Direct link** — You share the URL on Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, or email. Any student who clicks it lands on your page.

**Business cards and flyers** — Print your booking URL on physical materials. Students scan or type it and book instantly.

**DriveBook search** — When students search by suburb on `drivebook.com.au`, your profile appears in the results if you service their area.

**Google** — Your booking page is indexed by Google. As your reviews accumulate and your page ages, you may rank for suburb-specific searches like "driving instructor Maylands WA."

**Referrals** — When a student refers a friend, they share your link. The friend lands on your page, sees your reviews, and books.

## What Makes a Good Booking Page

The difference between a booking page that converts and one that doesn't usually comes down to three things:

1. **A professional profile photo** — Students want to see who they're getting in the car with
2. **A genuine bio** — Brief and specific beats generic. "Patient with nervous learners, familiar with all test centres in Perth" beats "Experienced instructor."
3. **Recent reviews** — Even five genuine reviews make a significant difference versus none. Ask students to review you after they pass.

## Customising Your Page

All customisation happens in your DriveBook dashboard:
- Upload your profile and car photos under Profile
- Write your bio under Profile → About
- Set your pricing under Settings → Pricing
- Configure your service area and languages under Settings → Availability
- On PRO, Studio, and Business plans: upload your logo and set brand colours under Branding
- On Studio and Business plans: connect your own custom domain under Settings → Domain

Your page updates immediately when you save changes — no waiting for deployment or technical steps.
