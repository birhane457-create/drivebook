A fair question before joining any platform is: if I ever want to leave, can I take my data with me?

The short answer for DriveBook is yes.

## What You Can Export

From your DriveBook dashboard, you can export:

**Student list** — Names, email addresses, and phone numbers for all students you've worked with on the platform.

**Booking history** — A record of all past lessons including dates, durations, and payment status.

**Earnings records** — Your income history by booking, useful for tax and accounting purposes.

**Progress notes** — Lesson feedback and notes recorded for each student.

These exports are available in standard formats (CSV for data, PDF for reports) that can be opened in any spreadsheet application or imported into other systems.

## What You Don't Own

Reviews are tied to your DriveBook profile and cannot be ported to another platform — they're associated with the DriveBook booking record that the student reviewed. However, the content of reviews is visible to you and you can reference them.

Payment processing history lives partly with Stripe. Your Stripe account (which DriveBook creates on your behalf for payouts) remains accessible to you — Stripe is your account, not DriveBook's.

## Why This Matters

Some platforms make data export difficult or impossible as a retention mechanism. DriveBook's position is that your student relationships are yours. You built them through your teaching. The platform facilitated the booking, but the trust between you and your students is not something DriveBook owns.

Practically, this means:
- You're not locked in by data hostage
- You can switch tools if a better option emerges
- Your business continuity doesn't depend entirely on DriveBook's continued operation

## How to Export

Log into your DriveBook dashboard, go to Settings → Data → Export. Select the data type and date range, and download the file.

For a full account export before closing an account, contact DriveBook support. A complete data package will be prepared and sent within a reasonable timeframe.
