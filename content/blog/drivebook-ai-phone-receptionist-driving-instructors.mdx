Most driving instructors miss calls during lessons. You're in the car, your phone rings, and you either ignore it or answer at an unsafe moment. The student calling has already found your number, wants to book, and is ready to commit — but if you don't answer, the chance of them calling back drops significantly.

DriveBook solves this with an AI receptionist that answers calls on your behalf, around the clock.

## What the AI Receptionist Does

When a student calls your DriveBook number, the AI receptionist:

- Greets them professionally
- Asks for their name, phone number, and pickup location
- Checks your available slots in real time
- Offers times that work
- Creates the booking and sends an SMS confirmation

The student hangs up with a confirmed lesson. You finish your current lesson and see the new booking in your dashboard. No missed calls, no callback tags, no admin time.

## How It Works

The AI is connected to your DriveBook availability calendar. It only offers slots you've marked as open — it can't accidentally double-book or offer times outside your working hours.

When a booking is created via the AI:
- The student receives an SMS confirmation with the lesson details
- The booking appears in your dashboard immediately
- Your calendar updates so no one else can book that slot
- A reminder SMS goes out to the student automatically before the lesson

## It Works in Any Language

The AI handles calls in English by default, and can detect and respond in other languages. For instructors who teach students who prefer Arabic, Mandarin, or other languages, this removes a barrier that would otherwise require a human receptionist.

## What It Doesn't Do

The AI receptionist is designed for booking, not for complex conversations. It handles:
- New lesson bookings
- Availability questions
- Basic pricing information

For anything more complex — like cancellations, complaints, or questions about specific test routes — it directs the caller to message you directly.

## What This Means for Your Revenue

Every missed call is a potential booking lost. For an instructor taking 30 lessons per week at $80/hr, a single lost booking is $80. Instructors who use DriveBook's AI answering service consistently fill their schedule faster than those relying on manual callbacks.

The AI doesn't take commissions, doesn't need days off, and doesn't need training. It reads your calendar and books accordingly.
