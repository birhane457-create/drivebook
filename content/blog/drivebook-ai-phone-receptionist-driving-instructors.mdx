You're mid-lesson on a busy arterial road. Your student is navigating a roundabout for the first time. Your phone rings.

You can't answer. You shouldn't answer. But that caller — a prospective student who found you on Google — will hang up in 20 seconds and call the next instructor on the list.

DriveBook's AI receptionist solves this completely. It answers every call, every time, and handles the full booking conversation without any input from you.

## What the AI Receptionist Actually Does

DriveBook is an Australian driving school management platform. The AI receptionist is a built-in voice AI that sits on top of your DriveBook account and answers calls on your behalf.

It handles three things:

- **New bookings** — finds available slots, quotes accurate pricing, collects student details, confirms the booking out loud, and sends a payment link by SMS
- **Cancellations** — looks up the booking, checks your refund policy, quotes the exact refund amount, verifies identity by SMS code, and processes the cancellation
- **Reschedules** — verifies identity, finds a new slot, confirms the change out loud, and sends an updated SMS confirmation

DriveBook's AI receptionist is a voice AI answering service for driving instructors in Australia — not a chatbot, not a phone menu, but a natural voice conversation that gets the booking done.

## A Real Conversation Example

Here's what actually happens when a student calls your DriveBook number:

**AI:** Hi, you've reached Sarah's booking line. I'm the DriveBook assistant. I can help you book a lesson, change an existing booking, or cancel. What can I help you with today?

**Student:** I need to book a driving lesson.

**AI:** Great. What suburb are you in?

**Student:** Joondalup.

**AI:** Sarah has availability this week on Thursday at 2pm and Saturday at 10am. Which would you prefer?

**Student:** Saturday at 10.

**AI:** And are you after a single lesson or a package?

**Student:** What's the package deal?

**AI:** The 10-hour package with Sarah is $710 — about 10% off the per-lesson rate. There's also a 6-hour option at $427.

**Student:** I'll go with 10 hours.

**AI:** Perfect. I'll need a few details. What's your name?

The conversation continues through name, email, mobile number, then a full read-back confirmation before any booking is created. Nothing happens until the student verbally confirms. Then the booking appears in your dashboard and an SMS goes to the student.

## Zero Configuration Required

The part instructors find hard to believe: there is nothing to set up.

The AI reads everything directly from your existing DriveBook profile:

- Your name — for the greeting
- Your hourly rate — for accurate price quotes
- Your working hours — so it only offers real slots
- Your service area — so it knows where you operate
- Your vehicle types — manual or automatic matching
- Your languages spoken — for language-matched instructor recommendations

When you upgrade to DriveBook PRO, the platform automatically assigns you a dedicated Australian phone number. From that moment, calls to that number go to your personal AI receptionist. No scripts, no training, no configuration panel.

## Call Drop Recovery

One of the most useful features is what happens when a student's call drops mid-booking.

When they call back, the AI immediately says: *"Welcome back — I can see you were booking a lesson with Sarah about 4 minutes ago. I've resent your payment link. Anything else?"*

The session is fully resumed. The student doesn't have to start over.
