When students search for a driving instructor, they'll often find you in one of two ways: a social media page or a dedicated website. The question of which to prioritise — and which builds more trust — has a reasonably clear answer.

## What a Social Media Page Does Well

Facebook and Instagram pages are free, quick to set up, and familiar to most people. They allow you to post updates, respond to messages, and collect some reviews. Many instructors use them as their primary online presence.

The advantages:

- Easy to create and manage
- Students can leave Facebook reviews
- You can post lesson tips, student pass photos, and updates
- Messaging is familiar and low-friction for many students

## What a Social Media Page Doesn't Do Well

**You don't own it** — Your Facebook page exists at Facebook's discretion. If they change their policies, reduce organic reach, or your account is flagged for any reason, your online presence is affected. You have no control.

**It doesn't rank as well on Google** — A Facebook page appears in Google results, but it doesn't rank as a dedicated website does for local searches. "Driving instructor Mandurah" is much more likely to surface a dedicated webpage than a Facebook profile.

**Booking is friction-heavy** — Facebook has booking tools, but they're clunky compared to a dedicated booking page. Most students end up messaging, then waiting for a reply, then going back and forth to confirm a time.

**It looks like the minimum effort** — A student comparing two instructors — one with only a Facebook page and one with a dedicated booking page — will often perceive the second as more professional and established, even if the first has 10 more years of experience.

## What a Custom Domain Does

A custom domain — `yourname.com.au` or `yourschoolname.com.au` — is an address you own. It doesn't depend on any social media platform's continued goodwill.

**Trust signals** — A custom domain signals that you've invested in your business. It's a small cost with a disproportionate trust benefit. Students and parents searching for instructors perceive `.com.au` businesses as more legitimate.

**Google indexing** — A dedicated URL gets indexed by Google independently. Over time, as reviews accumulate and the page ages, it can rank for suburb-specific searches.

**Professional email** — A custom domain enables a professional email address (`hello@yourdrivingschool.com.au`), which matters for parents of younger students who receive emails from you.

**Full control** — You decide what's on the page, how it looks, and what students see first.

## Do You Need Both?

Yes, and they serve different purposes:

**Social media** — Discovery, updates, student success posts, community. Good for people who are just learning about you.

**Your booking page** — Conversion. This is where people who are ready to commit go to book. This is where your custom domain should point.

Think of social media as the top of a funnel and your booking page as the bottom. They work best together.
