## 🎯 ACTUAL BOOKING FLOW (From Code)

### Step 1: Search by Location
- User enters: suburb, postcode, or full address
- Example: "Maylands WA", "6051", or "6/226 Whatley Cr Maylands WA 6051"
- System searches instructors who service that area

### Step 2: View Results
- Shows grid of instructor cards (3 columns on desktop)
- Each card shows: photo, name, rating, hourly rate, vehicle type
- "Book Now" button on each card

### Step 3: Select Instructor
- Click on instructor card
- View detailed profile with bio, car image, service areas
- See booking form with package options

### Step 4: Choose Package & Book
- Select package (single lesson or bulk hours)
- Enter pickup address
- Choose date/time
- Create account or login
- Complete payment

## 🎯 Technical Specifications for All Images

### Dimensions:
- **Desktop Hero**: 1920x1080px (16:9)
- **Mobile Hero**: 1080x1920px (9:16)
- **Carousel Slides**: 1200x800px (3:2)
- **Feature Cards**: 800x600px (4:3)
- **Instructor Cards**: 400x500px (portrait)

### Style Guidelines (Based on Your Interface):
- **Primary Color**: Purple (#7C3AED, #9333EA) - for buttons, accents
- **Secondary Color**: Green (#10B981) - for verification badges
- **Background**: White (#FFFFFF), Light Gray (#F3F4F6, #E5E7EB)
- **Text**: Dark Gray (#111827), Medium Gray (#6B7280)
- **Cards**: White with subtle shadows, rounded corners (12-16px radius)
- **Buttons**: Purple background, white text, rounded, hover effects

### Typography (Match Your Interface):
- **Headlines**: Bold, 32-48px, sans-serif
- **Names**: Bold, 20-24px
- **Subtext**: Regular, 14-16px, gray
- **Button Text**: Semibold, 16px, white

### UI Elements to Include:
- **Search Bar**: White background, rounded, purple search icon/button
- **Instructor Cards**: 
  - Photo at top (rounded corners)
  - Name below photo
  - Descriptor text (e.g., "female, friendly")
  - 5-star rating (gold stars)
  - Purple "Book Now" button
  - Subtle shadow and hover effect
- **Verification Badges**: 
  - Green background (#10B981)
  - White text
  - Checkmark icon
  - Rounded pill shape
- **Decorative Elements**:
  - Light purple circular blobs in corners
  - Subtle patterns
  - Clean, not cluttered

### Design Principles:
- Clean and modern (match your screenshot)
- Professional but friendly and approachable
- Trust-focused with verification badges
- Mobile-first thinking
- Accessibility-compliant contrast ratios
- Consistent with your existing interface
- Use real diverse instructor photos (different genders, ethnicities, ages)
