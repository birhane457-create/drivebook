# Master Question Bank — 22603VIC Certificate IV in Cyber Security

## BSBWHS309 — WHS

1. What is the WHS Act 2020 (WA) and who does it apply to?
2. Give an example of a psychological hazard in an IT role and how you would control it.
3. What is the hierarchy of controls? Give an IT example of each level.
4. What is an HSR and when must they be consulted?

## VU23217 — Cyber Security Fundamentals

5. What is the CIA triad? Give a real example of each principle being violated.
6. A company's website is slow or unreachable after a spike in traffic. What attack might this be and how does it work?
7. What is social engineering? Give three examples of techniques attackers use.
8. What is the NIST Cybersecurity Framework? Name the five functions.
9. What are the ACSC Essential Eight? Why is patching high priority?
10. Using DriveBook as an example, identify three assets that require protection and the threats to each.

## VU23213 — Networking

11. Explain the OSI model. At which layers do most attacks occur and why?
12. What is the difference between TCP and UDP? Which is more secure and why?
13. Explain a TCP SYN flood attack. How does a SYN cookie defence work?
14. What is the difference between a stateless packet filter and a stateful firewall?
15. How does HTTPS protect data in transit? Explain the TLS handshake in plain terms.
16. What is DNS poisoning? How does HTTPS protect against it even if DNS is compromised?
17. What is the difference between IPv4 private and public addresses? Give examples.
18. In your Railway logs you saw `fd12:edf3:d9c7:...`. What type of address is this and what does it mean?

## VU23215 — Security Testing

19. What is the OWASP Top 10? Name all 10 categories.
20. What is SQL injection? How do parameterised queries prevent it?
21. What is the difference between black box, white box, and grey box penetration testing?
22. You're asked to conduct a pen test on a client's system. What must you obtain before starting?
23. A user reports they can access another user's private data by changing a number in the URL. What vulnerability is this and how do you fix it?
24. What is CSRF (Cross-Site Request Forgery)? How do you prevent it?
25. What is the difference between vulnerability scanning and penetration testing?
26. Explain the penetration testing phases: Planning, Scanning, Exploitation, Reporting.

## VU23223 — Legislation and Privacy

33. What is the Spam Act 2003 and how does it apply to DriveBook's email notifications?
