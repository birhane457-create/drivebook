# Lab — Threat Modelling

## Tasks
1. List at least 10 DriveBook security-sensitive assets.
2. List relevant actors and their trust assumptions.
3. Draw a data-flow diagram for:
   learner → booking → payment → webhook → wallet → payout.
4. Mark every trust boundary.
5. Identify attack surfaces at each boundary.
6. Generate threats using STRIDE.
7. Select the five threats requiring the clearest controls.
8. For each, define a preventive and detective control.
9. Define a verification test for each control.
10. Conduct an oral defence of one threat from asset through verification.

## Deliverables
- DFD
- threat register
- control mapping
- verification plan
- completed `EVIDENCE.md`

Do not expose production secrets or customer information in the evidence.
